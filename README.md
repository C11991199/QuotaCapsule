**See your Codex quota without leaving your editor.**

Your five-hour and weekly limits sit beside the Windows system tray. Glance at the taskbar for the remaining percentage; right-click for reset times and other limits reported by your account.

**[Try every feature free for 7 days](https://colin-12.itch.io/quota-capsule#demo)** · No card details. No automatic charge.

## What you can do

- See both quota periods together, with local reset times and countdowns in details.
- Enable optional 20% and 5% low-quota alerts and confirmed recovery reminders.
- Choose a display, move the capsule left, and adjust text size.
- Use light or dark appearance and English or Chinese instructions.
- Extract and run a native portable Windows app. Keep the EXE and its .config together.

## Requirements and privacy

Windows 11 x64 with a horizontal taskbar is recommended. Sign in to Codex desktop or CLI with your ChatGPT account first. Subscription quota is not available through an API-key account. Access to chatgpt.com is required.

Quota requests go directly to chatgpt.com using your existing local Codex sign-in. There are no ads, telemetry, or developer backend. The app keeps bounded local diagnostic logs; do not publish authentication files, tokens, or private diagnostics in an issue.

The executable is unsigned. Included SHA-256 files check download integrity, not publisher identity. Readings depend on the account and service response; third-party changes may require updates. This app does not add quota or report general ChatGPT chat/image limits or OpenAI API balances.
